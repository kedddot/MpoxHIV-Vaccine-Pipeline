3. NEW PIPELINE STEPS WITH NO SECTION IN THE PAPER
================================================================================

3.1  PHASE 1H -- IEDB EXPERIMENTAL CORROBORATION            [deviation #22]
     Every epitope in this construct is PREDICTED. Nothing in the paper's
     Section I asks whether any has been observed in a laboratory. Phase 1H
     cross-references all 32 construct epitopes against IEDB's curated assay
     records.
     RESULT: 26/32 (81%) corroborated -- 10 EXPERIMENTALLY CONFIRMED (exact
     match or contained in a curated epitope, with assay data), 16 OVERLAP a
     curated epitope by >=8 aa, 6 NOVEL. Assay classes: MHC-ligand elution
     mass spectrometry, T-cell assays, B-cell assays.
     TWO QUALIFICATIONS THAT MUST TRAVEL WITH THAT NUMBER:
       (a) 14 of 26 are CROSS-SPECIES orthopoxvirus (vaccinia) orthologs.
           IEDB holds 29 Monkeypox-virus epitopes versus 17,903 vaccinia ones,
           and A35R/B5R/L1R have direct vaccinia orthologs -- orthopoxvirus
           cross-reactivity is the mechanism by which smallpox vaccination
           protects against mpox. Excluding them would discard nearly the whole
           relevant experimental record, but they must be labelled.
       (b) Every match was verified against our own Phase 1A reference variants
           (83-100% identity, zero rejected), because a 9-mer can match by
           chance in a ~200 kb poxvirus proteome and UniProt's orthopoxvirus
           gene names ("Ankyrin repeat protein OPG189") do not resemble ours
           ("B5R"), so a name-based check would reject genuine orthologs.
     NOVEL IS NOT A DEFECT. Predicting epitopes with no prior curation is the
     point of a prediction pipeline. This step changed no selection.

3.2  CUMULATIVE POPULATION COVERAGE                         [deviation #23]
     Section II.C.I.F computes coverage PER EPITOPE and applies the >=90% gate
     to each. That is a prioritisation signal, not the vaccine's coverage. A
     recipient is covered if ANY epitope binds one of their alleles, so the
     correct figure is computed over the UNION of binding alleles -- which is
     what IEDB's own Population Coverage tool does for a multi-epitope set.
     THE DIFFERENCE IS LARGE AND RUNS IN THE VACCINE'S FAVOUR: individually
     only 4 of 23 HLA-restricted epitopes clear 90%. Cumulatively both classes
     exceed 99.99%. See 5.1 for how to report this honestly.

3.3  ALPHAFOLD FOLD-CONFIDENCE ANALYSIS                     [deviation #21]
     Section II.C.II.D validates the model with MolProbity only. MolProbity
     scores LOCAL STEREOCHEMISTRY -- a clean-geometry random coil passes every
     one of its criteria. AlphaFold's own confidence output is now analysed
     separately: pTM, PAE, fraction_disordered, ranking_score, and pairwise
     cross-model CA RMSD across all five ranked models.
     THIS IS THE MOST CONSEQUENTIAL ADDITION IN THIS FILE -- it changes what
     Phase III is allowed to claim. See 5.3.

3.4  PHASE 3 STEP A -- RECEPTOR AND LIGAND PREPARATION
     Section III.A says only that TLR-2 (6NIG) and TLR-4 (8WTA) were "taken
     from the RCSB Protein Data Bank" and docked. Three things in the raw
     depositions would corrupt a docking run and none is mentioned:
       * 6NIG IS A CRYSTALLISATION CHIMERA. Its "TLR2" entity is a TLR2
         ectodomain fused to variable lymphocyte receptor B (VLR-B) from the
         hagfish Eptatretus stoutii. From the deposition's own _struct_ref_seq:
         TLR2 (UniProt O60603) = author residues 1-507; VLR-B (Q2YE02) =
         509-576; residue 508 is an engineered linker and IS modelled. Docking
         the unstripped chain would let the vaccine dock onto hagfish protein
         and be scored as a human TLR2 interaction.
       * 6NIG HAS AN AGONIST IN THE TARGET SITE. Ligand KQD is Diprovocim, a
         synthetic small-molecule TLR2 agonist occupying the exact pocket the
         vaccine is meant to dock into. Left in place it blocks the site.
       * HADDOCK v2.4 DOES NOT ACCEPT mmCIF. RCSB and AlphaFold Server both
         return .cif; every prepared input is written as legacy .pdb.
     8WTA needs no such surgery, but MD-2's lipid-A acyl chains are retained
     as a DELIBERATE, FLAGGED decision -- that pocket is functionally occupied
     in the activated receptor and emptying it would present an artificial
     cavity. Prepared: TLR2 chain A residues 27-507 (478 residues); TLR4
     chains A+C (TLR4 27-627 + MD-2 19-158, 741 residues).

3.5  HUMAN SELF-HOMOLOGY AS AN EXECUTED STEP (Phase 1Ec)
     The paper describes this in II.C.I.E but folds it into the toxicity/
     allergenicity paragraph. It is a separate executed step with its own
     output. Result: 139/139 candidates Self_Homology_Status = NONE, so no
     autoimmune-mimicry liability in the pool.

4. STATED RULES THAT THE IMPLEMENTATION CHANGED
================================================================================
Each has a numbered deviation with full reasoning and, where applicable, a
citation. These are the ones a reviewer comparing methods to results will find.

4.1  TOXICITY: raw score -> evidence required               [deviation #1]
     A peptide is "toxic" only on a real BLAST hit or MERCI motif, not a raw
     ToxinPred score. The raw score alone called human albumin, human GAPDH, a
     plain linker AND our own beta-defensin-3 adjuvant toxic. Backed by
     ToxinPred2's own paper (Sharma et al. 2022, already ref #62).

4.2  SOURCE-PROTEIN STABILITY: filter -> flag               [deviation #2]
     II.C.I.B says <40 instability is required. All 27 Mpox A35R variants
     scored 41.03, which silently deleted an entire required antigen. Now
     advisory; the short peptides get their own check at 2A. OUR OWN JUDGMENT
     CALL, no citation -- say so.

4.3  MHC-II ALLELE PANEL: 5 -> 21                           [deviation #8]
     Phase 1F scores coverage across DRB1+DQ+DP but Phase 1D originally tested
     DRB1 only, so 11 of 21 alleles could never register as bound -- including
     HLA-DPB1*05:01, the highest-frequency allele in the MHC-II panel. Note
     honestly that a wider panel also makes the 90% gate easier to reach; the
     fix was made for internal consistency, not to pass the gate.

4.4  FoldX: ddG -> Total Folding Energy                     [deviation #10]
     II.C.II.C lists "destabilizing ddG predictions (FoldX web) > +1.5
     kcal/mol for candidate surface mutations" as a rejection criterion. ddG is
     by definition a difference between a mutant and a wild type, and THIS
     STUDY HAS NO DESIGNED MUTANTS. The quantity has no referent. The value is
     reported as FoldX_Total_Energy_kcal_mol and is contextual only. The
     "+1.5 kcal/mol" trigger must be removed from the methodology.

4.5  PHENIX ORDERING: 2C, not 2D                            [deviation #11]
     II.C.II.D places minimisation/repair after MolProbity. Refinement runs in
     Step 2C so that Step 2D validates the refined coordinates. Validating
     before refining would report the quality of a structure that is then
     discarded.

4.6  JUNCTION-CYSTEINE RULE: adjuvant exempt                [deviation #12]
     ***FLAG THIS ONE TO A REVIEWER.*** The paper mandates beta-defensin 3 as
     the adjuvant AND a ">2 cysteines per 8-20 aa junction peptide" rejection
     rule. Mature beta-defensin-3 natively carries SIX cysteines, so the two
     requirements cannot both be satisfied. Resolved in the direction that
     preserves the adjuvant the paper requires: 3 adjuvant-overlapping
     junctions are exempted and reported explicitly. Verified for the current
     construct that ZERO non-adjuvant junctions violate the rule.

4.7  POPULATION COVERAGE: required -> prioritised           [deviation #9]
     The paper's own wording is "PRIORITIZED as population-covering
     candidates", but the code was deleting sub-90% peptides. Measured
     cumulative MHC-II coverage tops out below 90%, so the hard filter produced
     a construct with ZERO helper-T epitopes -- no GPGPG linkers, no CD4+ help,
     and research questions 8 and 9 unanswerable. Now retained and ranked
     lower. This IS an interpretive choice; disclose it.

4.8  ALLERGENICITY: either-predictor -> consensus            [deviation #24]
     II.C.I.E treats AllerTOP and AllergenFP as independent exclusion signals.
     Measured on the real pool the two DISAGREE on 41 of the 70 peptides where
     both can run (59%) and agree on ALLERGEN for only 14 -- so a single
     positive was excluding peptides on close to a coin flip, and it cost
     HIV_gp41 every one of its B-cell candidates. Exclusion now requires both
     to agree. Where only one predictor applies (AllergenFP cannot run below
     16 aa, which is every MHC-I and MHC-II peptide here) that one still
     decides alone. QN_Ratio > 0.30 still excludes unconditionally.
     NOTE: this brings Section I.E into line with Section II.A.a, which ALREADY
     specifies a two-predictor consensus rule for the assembled construct. The
     paper was internally inconsistent on this point.

4.9  CAMSOL AT 2A: score, not patch length                  [deviation #25]
     II.C.II.A.b says "constructs with long (> 8 aa) high-scoring aggregation
     patches were flagged" for CamSol. CamSol INTRINSIC does not report a patch
     length -- its headline output is "the protein variant intrinsic solubility
     score", which is what was recorded for every construct in this project. A
     score in CamSol's roughly -3..+3 range cannot reach 8, so that check never
     once fired. Now interpreted on CamSol's own published scale (Sormanni,
     Aprile & Vendruscolo, J Mol Biol 2015): < -1 poorly soluble, > +1 highly
     soluble. TRUE patch length is measured at Step 2C from CamSol STRUCTURAL,
     which does report one -- so the paper should specify WHICH CamSol mode
     applies at which step.

4.10 BigMHC NEGATIVE SCORES                                 [deviation #19]
     BigMHC IM emits negative values for weak peptides; read as a bounded
     ranking signal, not a probability. Deprioritised, never excluded --
     consistent with the paper's own wording.

4.11 EPITOPE DEDUPLICATION IS CROSS-CLASS
     Not stated anywhere in the paper, but it is the single most consequential
     selection rule in Phase 1G: a candidate sharing a sequence frame with
     anything already selected is skipped, GLOBALLY across targets and classes,
     and that check runs BEFORE any priority ranking. It is correct behaviour
     (do not count one antigenic region twice) and it explains the gp41 result
     in 5.2. It must be described in II.C.I.G.

5. WHERE THE RESULTS CONTRADICT A STATED METHODOLOGY SENTENCE

These are not additions. They are places where the paper as written says

5.1  "PHILIPPINE POPULATION COVERAGE" IS NOT WHAT WAS COMPUTED  [deviation #7]
     The paper repeatedly frames coverage as tailored to Philippine-prevalent
     strains and Metro Manila. The STRAIN targeting is genuine -- CRF01_AE and
     Clade II sequences were used. THE HLA COVERAGE IS NOT PHILIPPINE.
     AFND has no usable Philippine table: the Philippines National Capital
     Region set (n=51) is 1-FIELD ONLY (A*02, not A*02:01) and therefore
     unusable; the only 2-field Philippine data is "Philippines Ivatan"
     (n=50), an indigenous Batanes population not representative of lowland
     Filipinos; and HLA-DQB1 and HLA-DPB1 have NO Philippine data at any
     resolution -- those two loci carry most of the MHC-II coverage.
     MUST BE DESCRIBED AS "SOUTHEAST ASIAN (AUSTRONESIAN) PROXY COVERAGE",
     NOT "Philippine population coverage", everywhere it appears.
     Note this does NOT affect Section III.E, where the C-ImmSim HLA profiles
     are named individually and can remain as specified.

5.2  SEVEN ANTIGENS ARE CLAIMED; SIX ARE UNIQUELY REPRESENTED
     Epitopes per source antigen in the final construct:
       HIV_p24 7 | Mpox_A35R 6 | Mpox_B5R 6 | Mpox_L1R 6 | HIV_gp41 5 |
       HIV_gp120 2 | HIV_p17 0 unique
     HIV_p17 contributes no unique epitope -- its two are shared with p24,
     both being domains of the same Gag polyprotein. Defensible, but state it.
     SEPARATELY: HIV_gp41 contributes ZERO B-CELL EPITOPES. Its MPER region
     carries the best-characterised broadly neutralising antibody epitopes in
     HIV, so this is a real gap and a reviewer will find it. Cause is known
     precisely: gp41's entire surviving B-cell pool consists of overlapping
     windows of ONE region (SLKPCVKLTP), every one of which is blocked by
     cross-class frame deduplication against gp41's own selected epitopes
     KPCVKLTPL and SLWDQSLKP (MHC-I) and PIPIHYCTPAGYAIL (MHC-II). The
     underlying limitation is that the TOP-10 per-antigen shortlist has no
     DIVERSITY requirement: gp41 spent ten slots on two regions while
     Mpox_B5R's ten covered nine. Fixing it needs frame-diversified
     shortlisting plus roughly 50-100 additional manual allergenicity
     submissions and was NOT done. State it as a limitation.

5.3  "DOCK THE ALPHAFOLD3 MODEL OF THE VACCINE" IS NOT SUPPORTABLE AS WRITTEN
                                                            [deviation #21]
     Section III.A specifies docking the whole vaccine model into TLR-2 and
     TLR-4. Measured on the actual model:
                              Adjuvant (1-45)   Epitope cassettes (51-566)
       Mean pLDDT                  91.3              21.4 - 39.7
       Residues at pLDDT >= 70     100%              0%
       Intra-region PAE            2.41 A            22.1 - 29.0 A
       Cross-model CA RMSD         0.13 A            18.5 - 23.9 A
     Whole model pTM 0.16-0.17 across ALL FIVE ranked models; 84.6% of
     residues below pLDDT 50; the only 46 residues reaching pLDDT >= 70 form
     ONE contiguous run, 1-46 -- exactly the beta-defensin-3 adjuvant.
     Inter-region PAE is ~31 A against a ~31.75 A ceiling, i.e. AlphaFold
     cannot place the cassettes relative to anything.
     Docking the whole model means docking ONE ARBITRARY CONFORMER of a
     ~520-residue coil: substituting any other ranked model moves those
     regions by 18-24 A, so the docking result would not reproduce.
     THIS IS ARCHITECTURE, NOT A MODELLING FAILURE. Flexible GPGPG/AAY/KK
     linkers are chosen precisely so each epitope stays independently
     accessible; a determined global fold is not the design goal. No epitope
     reselection changes it, and MD will not fold ~520 de novo residues.
     WHAT TO WRITE INSTEAD: Phase III docks residues 1-45. This is not a
     fallback -- human beta-defensin 3 IS the TLR-1/2 and TLR-4 agonist and is
     the reason the adjuvant is in the construct at all. The epitope cassettes
     have no proposed receptor interaction to model. The region AlphaFold
     determines confidently is exactly the region the docking claim depends on.
     KNOCK-ON EFFECTS: research question 4 should be scoped to the adjuvant
     domain; questions 5 and 6 (100/500 ns MD, RMSF, Rg, eigenvalues,
     deformability) apply to the adjuvant-receptor complex, not the whole
     construct; and the Section IV daughter-construct replication inherits the
     same limit.

5.4  MOLPROBITY ALONE DOES NOT ESTABLISH "STEREOCHEMICAL QUALITY"
     Research question 3 asks for stereochemical quality "specifically
     regarding its Ramachandran-favored regions". The construct returns
     MolProbity 0.90 (EXCELLENT band 0.5-1.5) and 97.16% Ramachandran-favored,
     which meets the paper's stated target of 95-98%. Report those.
     BUT the MolProbity web report also returns, in red:
       Favored rotamers   91.54%  (goal >98%)
       Cis nonProlines    0.39%   (goal <0.05%)
       CA Geometry outliers 2.31% (goal <0.5%)
       CaBLAM outliers    3.2%    (goal <1.0%)
     while everything Phenix restrains during refinement is perfect (bad bonds
     0/4468, bad angles 0/6052, Cbeta deviations 0.00%, clashscore 0.68 at the
     99th percentile). The MolProbity SCORE is computed from only clashscore,
     rotamer outliers and Ramachandran outliers, with built-in tolerances --
     CaBLAM, CA geometry and cis-peptides are not in the formula at all, which
     is why the headline number does not reflect them.
     LOCALISED: 59 of the 62 flagged residues lie OUTSIDE the adjuvant, at mean
     pLDDT 29.2 (whole model 35.7; adjuvant 91.3). All 13 CaBLAM outliers are
     in the epitope cassettes, zero in the adjuvant; 12 of 13 CA-geometry
     outliers likewise. They cluster in runs (341-346, 364-369) that coincide
     with GPGPG linker regions.
     This is independent corroboration of 5.3 from a tool built for exactly
     this purpose (CaBLAM is MolProbity's "low-resolution criteria" section,
     designed for structures whose detailed geometry cannot be trusted).
     Report the red rows rather than only the score.

5.5  "SEQUENCES FLAGGED ONLY WITH PREDICTED INSOLUBLE WERE REJECTED"
                                                            [deviation #15]
     Section II.C.II.A.b states this outright. THE SHIPPED CONSTRUCT IS
     PREDICTED INSOLUBLE (DeepSol S2 = 0.4443739) and was carried forward, not
     rejected. This is the WEAKEST deviation in the project and the sentence
     must be rewritten before submission.
     The defensible framing, now stronger than it was: the two sequence-based
     solubility predictors DISAGREE. CamSol intrinsic scores the same sequence
     0.865041 -- comfortably on the soluble side, and the highest of any
     construct generated in this project -- while DeepSol calls it insoluble.
     Neither structure-aware tool corroborates insolubility either: the
     Aggrescan3D patch (16 aa) and the CamSol structural patch (7 aa) both sit
     at mean pLDDT ~22, i.e. in regions AlphaFold cannot place. Under the
     paper's OWN compound rule (II.C.II.C: reject when DeepSol < 0.50 AND
     Aggrescan3D finds a > 8 aa patch in a pLDDT >= 70 region) the construct is
     NOT rejected, because no confident patch exists. Cite that compound rule
     as the operative one and present DeepSol alone as a flag.
     Also note: shortening does not fix it. A 393 aa length-lever variant
     scored 0.393, WORSE than the longer constructs.

5.6  HYDROPHOBIC SURFACE FLAGS ARE A DISORDER SIGNATURE     [deviation #18]
     II.C.II.C sets hydrophobic surface fraction > 0.25 and exposed hydrophobic
     patch area > 250 A^2 as structural flags. Measured: 0.409 and 649.3 A^2,
     both over. But both are computed by SASA over a model that is 84.6%
     disordered, so they describe one arbitrary conformer of a coil rather than
     a stable exposed surface. Report them as a disorder finding consistent
     with 5.3 and 5.4, not as an aggregation liability.

5.7  SEQUENCE COUNTS DIFFER FROM THE STATED DESIGN
     "30 variants" per antigen (II.C.I.A): actual retrieval was 202 of a
     targeted 210. HIV gp120/gp41/p17/p24 and Mpox L1R returned 30 each;
     Mpox_A35R returned 27 and Mpox_B5R 25, because NCBI holds no more
     non-redundant Clade II records. A DATA LIMIT, not a filter artefact.
     Related: II.C.I.A's clade filter originally required "Clade IIa/IIb" in
     the record, which returned ZERO B5R and A35R sequences -- NCBI genuinely
     lacks clade metadata for these. Sequences are now tagged confirmed/
     unconfirmed and both are kept [deviation #4].

5.8  ESM-2 TRAINING SET IS 774, NOT 500+500                 [deviation #5]
     II.C.I.C states the threshold was validated against "500 antigenic
     proteins from IEDB; 500 non-antigenic housekeeping proteins". The actual
     curated set is 774 real sequences (390 antigenic / 384 non-antigenic) --
     no synthetic data was generated to reach the stated count. Held-out
     accuracy 98.6%. Report the real number and let the held-out score carry
     the argument.
     Also worth stating: Phase 1C returned 167/167 ANTIGENIC with ZERO
     disagreements between ESM-2 and Kolaskar-Tongaonkar. A 100% pass rate
     means this step discriminates nothing at the source-protein level, which
     is expected because all seven are known immunogens. Present it as a
     consistency check between two methods, not as a filter.



5.9  ANTIGEN PROVENANCE IS WRONG IN TWO WAYS      [deviation #26 -- NEW, MAJOR]

     a protein identity, and two separate defects follow. Both were found while
     preparing native folds for SEMA-3D -- the first step that ever needed one
     structure per antigen. Neither is caused by SEMA.

     (A) THE FOUR HIV ANTIGENS ARE TWO POLYPROTEINS.
         HIV_gp120_Var_01 and HIV_gp41_Var_01 are the same 856-aa Env record
         (YES72107.1); HIV_p17_Var_01 and HIV_p24_Var_01 are the same 1437-aa
         Gag-Pol (YES72110.1). Every epitope inherited its query's label
         regardless of where in the polyprotein it lies, so 9 of the 14 HIV
         epitopes in the construct are mislabelled.
