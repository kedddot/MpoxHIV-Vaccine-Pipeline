4. POPULATION COVERAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per-epitope coverage is the manuscript's >=90% prioritization criterion and is
NOT the vaccine's coverage. A recipient is covered if ANY epitope binds one of
their alleles, so the correct figure is computed over the UNION of binding
alleles across the construct.

  CUMULATIVE MHC-I    >99.99%   (union of 65 alleles across 13 epitopes)
  CUMULATIVE MHC-II   >99.99%   (union of 32 alleles across 10 epitopes)
  COMBINED            >99.99%

PER-LOCUS BREAKDOWN -- REPORT THIS ALONGSIDE THE COMBINED FIGURE:
  HLA-A     72.75%   (16/22 alleles bound)   <-- weakest locus
  HLA-B     97.04%   (31/33)
  HLA-C     99.64%   (18/19)
  HLA-DPB1  86.97%   (8/8)
  HLA-DQB1  95.25%   (6/7)
  HLA-DRB1  99.54%   (18/18)

The across-loci formula multiplies complements, so three loci above ~85% force
the combined value over 99.9% almost regardless of the rest. Quoted alone the
combined number is nearly uninformative and reads as overreach. HLA-A at 72.75%
is the honest headline. No locus saturates, so the figure is not an artefact.

THIS IS SOUTHEAST ASIAN (AUSTRONESIAN) PROXY COVERAGE, NOT PHILIPPINE.
Allele frequencies come from AFND, population "Singapore Riau Malay"
(pop_id 2042, n=132, 107 alleles across all six loci). AFND's Philippine
National Capital Region table is 1-field only (A*02, not A*02:01) and HLA-DQB1
and HLA-DPB1 have no Philippine data at any resolution. Cite AFND
(Gonzalez-Galarza et al., Nucleic Acids Research 2020, 48:D783-8), not IEDB.

5. EXPERIMENTAL CORROBORATION (IEDB)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All epitopes here are computationally predicted. Cross-referencing the final 32
against IEDB's curated assay records establishes which also have published
laboratory support.

  10  EXPERIMENTALLY CONFIRMED (exact match or contained within a curated
      epitope, with assay data attached)
  16  OVERLAP a curated epitope by >=8 aa
   6  NOVEL -- no curated match
  ----
  26/32 (81%) corroborated

Assay classes represented: MHC-ligand elution mass spectrometry, T-cell assays,
B-cell assays. Species split: 12 same-species, 14 cross-species orthopoxvirus
(vaccinia) orthologs.

TWO QUALIFICATIONS THAT MUST TRAVEL WITH THIS NUMBER:
(a) Vaccinia matches are CROSS-SPECIES. IEDB holds 29 Monkeypox-virus epitopes
    versus 17,903 vaccinia ones, and A35R/B5R/L1R have direct vaccinia
    orthologs -- orthopoxvirus cross-reactivity is the mechanism by which
    smallpox vaccination protects against mpox. Excluding them would discard
    nearly the entire relevant experimental record, but they must be labelled.
(b) Every match was verified against our own Phase 1A reference antigens
    (83-100% identity, zero rejected). This matters because a 9-mer can match
    by chance in a ~200 kb poxvirus proteome, and because UniProt's names for
    orthopoxvirus genes ("Ankyrin repeat protein OPG189") bear no resemblance
    to ours ("B5R"), so a name-based check would have rejected real orthologs.

NOVEL IS NOT A DEFECT. Predicting epitopes with no prior curation is the point
of a prediction pipeline. This analysis distinguishes the two; it did not rank
them, and it changed no selection.

  perfect (bonds, angles, Cbeta, clashscore), while everything describing
  whether the backbone is in a BELIEVABLE CONFORMATION is poor. Refinement made
  the geometry ideal without making the fold correct.

  This is an INDEPENDENT corroboration of Section 7, from criteria built for
  exactly this case -- CaBLAM is MolProbity's "low-resolution" section,
  designed for structures whose detailed geometry cannot be trusted.

  clashscore 0.40 and 0.00% poor rotamers, so this construct is marginally

7. FOLD CONFIDENCE -- THE FINDING THAT SETS PHASE III SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MolProbity scores LOCAL STEREOCHEMISTRY. It says nothing about whether the fold
is correct: a random coil with clean geometry passes every one of its criteria.
(For reference: the superseded 484 aa construct scored MolProbity 0.64 with
clashscore 0.40 and 0.00% poor rotamers, so this construct is marginally worse
on local geometry while remaining comfortably inside the EXCELLENT band.)
AlphaFold's own confidence output was therefore analysed separately.

                          Adjuvant (1-45)     Epitope cassettes (51-566)
  Mean pLDDT                   91.3                21.4 - 39.7
  Residues at pLDDT >= 70      100%                0%
  Intra-region PAE             2.41 A              22.1 - 29.0 A
  Cross-model CA RMSD          0.13 A              18.5 - 23.9 A
  (10 model pairs)

  Whole model: pTM 0.16-0.17 across ALL FIVE ranked models
               84.6% of residues below pLDDT 50
               only 46 residues reach pLDDT >= 70, forming ONE contiguous
               run (1-46) -- exactly the beta-defensin-3 adjuvant
               inter-region PAE ~31 A against a ~31.75 A ceiling

Docking the whole model would mean docking one arbitrary conformer of a
~520-residue coil: substituting any other ranked model moves those regions by
18-24 A, so the result would not reproduce.

THIS IS ARCHITECTURE, NOT A MODELLING FAILURE. Flexible GPGPG/AAY/KK linkers
are chosen precisely so each epitope stays independently accessible to
processing machinery; a determined global fold is not the design goal. No
epitope reselection changes it, and molecular dynamics will not fold ~520 de
novo residues.

CONSEQUENCE: Phase III docks residues 1-45. This is not a fallback -- human
beta-defensin-3 IS the TLR-1/2 and TLR-4 agonist, and is the reason the
adjuvant is in the construct at all. The epitope cassettes have no proposed
receptor interaction to model. The region AlphaFold determines confidently is
exactly the region the docking claim depends on.

THRESHOLDS ARE EXTERNAL CONVENTION, NOT MANUSCRIPT CRITERIA: pLDDT >= 70 is
AlphaFold's own confident band; pTM >= 0.5 the usual global-fold line; cross-
model RMSD <= 2.0 A the conventional "same structure" agreement. The manuscript
specifies no fold-confidence criterion at all.

8. HOW TO READ THE REVIEW VERDICTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
None of the following rejected the construct, and none is resolvable by a later
phase. They are limits to disclose, not defects to fix.

(a) DeepSol 0.4444 < 0.50 -- the sole failing criterion at both 2A and 2C.
    A PREDICTION, not a measurement, and it is contradicted by the other
    sequence-based predictor used here: CamSol intrinsic scores the same
    sequence 0.865, comfortably on the soluble side. Two solubility predictors
    disagree. Only wet-lab expression can settle it; no computational phase can.

(b) Hydrophobic fraction 0.409 (>0.25) and exposed hydrophobic area 649 A^2
    (>250) -- both computed by SASA over the AlphaFold model. That model is
    84.6% disordered, so these quantities describe one arbitrary conformer of a
    coil, not a stable exposed surface. They are a DISORDER signature, not an
    aggregation signature.

(c) Aggrescan3D patch 16 aa (>8 aa threshold) -- sits at mean pLDDT 22.0, i.e.
    in a region AlphaFold cannot place. Not confidently corroborated. The
    CamSol structural patch (7 aa) is under threshold. Both fall in disordered
    regions, which is the same finding as (b) reached by an independent route.

(d) Surface-charge junction flags (many, mostly at KK junctions) -- the KK
    linker IS two lysines, so any peptide spanning one is charge-rich by
    construction. Largely circular; advisory only.

(e) HemoPI2 hemolysis score 0.72 (>=0.50) -- not a rejection under the
    manuscript's conjunctive rule, which also requires hydrophobic fraction
    >0.60 (measured 0.401). WORTH STATING PLAINLY ANYWAY: beta-defensin-3 is a
    natural cationic antimicrobial peptide and is membrane-active by design, so
    a hemolysis signal is biologically expected from the adjuvant. It is the
    one flag that would benefit from experimental follow-up.

(f) 571 junction peptides carry composition-only toxicity flags with ZERO BLAST
    or MERCI evidence -- exactly the false-positive mode that the evidence-based
    toxicity rule was adopted to suppress. Advisory.

(g) Junction-level allergenicity is not automated (manual submission burden
    scales with candidate x junction count). A feasibility limitation.

(h) MolProbity's red rows -- favored rotamers 91.54%, cis nonProlines 0.39%,
    CA geometry outliers 2.31%, CaBLAM outliers 3.2%. Not blockers and not
    fixable by further refinement: 59 of 62 flagged residues sit in regions
    AlphaFold cannot place (mean pLDDT 29.2). They are the same disorder
    finding as (b) and (c), reached by a fourth independent route.

9. KNOWN GAPS -- STATE THESE, DO NOT LET A REVIEWER FIND THEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) HIV_gp41 contributes ZERO B-cell epitopes. Its MPER region carries the
    best-characterised broadly neutralising antibody epitopes in HIV, so this
    is a real gap. Cause is now known precisely: gp41's entire surviving B-cell
    pool consists of overlapping windows of ONE region (SLKPCVKLTP), every one
    of which is blocked by cross-class frame deduplication against gp41's own
    selected epitopes KPCVKLTPL, SLWDQSLKP (MHC-I) and PIPIHYCTPAGYAIL (MHC-II).
    Frame deduplication is CORRECT behaviour -- it prevents counting one
    antigenic region twice. The underlying limitation is that the TOP-10
    per-antigen shortlist has no diversity requirement: gp41 spent ten slots on
    two regions while Mpox_B5R's ten covered nine. Fixing this requires
    frame-diversified shortlisting plus roughly 50-100 additional manual
    allergenicity submissions, and was not done.

(2) HIV_p17 contributes no unique epitope (shared with p24; both Gag domains).
    SEE ALSO deviation #26: this is worse than recorded. The four HIV targets
    are really two polyprotein records, so 9 of the 14 HIV epitopes carry the
    wrong Target label. True per-antigen representation is 4 gp120 / 3 gp41 /
    1 p17 / 6 p24, not the labelled 4/3/6/1, and the antigen contributing no
    B-cell epitope is gp120, not gp41. Separately, Mpox_B5R is not the intended
    antigen at all: QJQ40305.1 is 100% OPG189, an ankyrin-repeat protein, and
    only 25.2% identical to OPG190/B6R, the actual vaccinia-B5R ortholog and
    EEV glycoprotein.

(3) SEMA 2.0 conformational B-cell screening did not run against the hosted
    service, which sits behind a commercial anti-bot WAF (re-verified endpoint
    by endpoint on 2026-08-28: the OpenAPI schema is served, but every
    functional endpoint returns a JavaScript challenge). RESOLVED 2026-08-29
    by running SEMA LOCALLY from source, as SEMA-3D on native antigen folds --
    which is what Sec. I.D actually specifies; the earlier implementation used
    the sequence-only SEMA-1D. See deviation #20.
    THE PREVIOUS "ZERO EFFECT" CLAIM HERE WAS WRONG and has been withdrawn. It
    asserted that frame deduplication rather than priority determines every
    B-cell slot. Re-derived from the shipped Phase 1F pool under Phase 1G's own
    screens, Mpox_B5R has SEVEN distinct B-cell candidates for two slots and
    Mpox_L1R has THREE for two, so three of the nine B-cell slots were
    contestable and a SEMA score could have arbitrated them. Measured envelope
    (Phase1G_sensitivity.py, replaying 1G's own objective at RNG_SEED=20260824):
    corroborating the currently-selected epitopes leaves the construct 100%
    identical, while corroborating only the unselected alternatives yields a
    587-aa construct at 27.2% identity with both T-cell blocks changed.
    MEASURED WITH REAL DATA (2026-08-29): SEMA-3D was run on AlphaFold models of
    all seven native antigens and its real calls replayed through Phase 1G ->
    6f34b53e, 566 aa, 100.0% identity, UNCHANGED. The gap is closed and the
    shipped construct stands. Of the nine B-cell epitopes, 1 is structurally
    corroborated (ATVYGDKIQGKNKRKR, A35R, 56.2% overlap), 6 are not, and 2
    cannot be scored because they are OPG053 rather than L1R -- see (2).

(4) The global fold is undetermined (Section 7). Phase III is scoped to
    residues 1-45 accordingly.

(5) Predicted solubility is unresolved and the two predictors disagree
    (Section 8a).

  beta-defensin-3 + EAAAK + 13 MHC-I (AAY) + 10 MHC-II (GPGPG) + 9 B-cell (KK),

  charge +40. Zero >80% hydrophobic windows, zero spurious linker motifs, zero

  "FAVORABLE PROTEIN FOLDING" NEEDS QUALIFYING -- see RQ3 and Section 7. The

  97.16% Ramachandran-favored, inside the paper's stated 95-98% target.
